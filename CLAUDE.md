## Notes and explanations
**Always use the `lld-notes` skill** (`.claude/skills/lld-notes/SKILL.md`) when writing or editing `*_Notes.md` files or explaining any concept. It holds the full rules:
- Explain one topic **completely** (Definition, then What problem it solves / why we study it, then Examples) before moving to the next topic.
- Put **important interview questions at the END**, then a one-line revision.
- Tables: closed boxes of `-` and `|` only (no `+`), made with `tools/notes_tools.py`.
- Class diagrams: plain ASCII, generated with `tools/notes_tools.py`.
